Ask us on WhatsApp
What can I request a quote for?

We take enquiries for kitchen cabinetry, wardrobes and storage, and TV units and media walls. Select the closest option when you start a quote.

What should I include in my first message?

Share your town or suburb, the room, rough dimensions if available, and what you need from the cabinetry. You can attach room photos or design references after WhatsApp opens.

Can I enquire about an existing kitchen or room?

Yes. Tell us what you’d like to keep, what you’d like to replace and whether the current layout needs to change.

How are pricing and lead times confirmed?

They depend on the layout, measurements, finishes, hardware and installation needs. The team can confirm pricing and timing once the scope is clear.

How do I check whether you can come to my area?

Include your suburb or town in your enquiry or call us on 083 338 9786 to discuss availability.

Does the website send the WhatsApp message for me?

No. The assistant and quotation wizard prepare your message. Review it in WhatsApp and press Send when you’re ready.
