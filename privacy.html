How your information is handled when you use this website.

Preparing a message

The website’s quotation wizard and automated assistant prepare a message in your browser. The information you enter is not submitted to the site or kept when you leave the page.

Contacting us

When you open WhatsApp or email, you choose whether to send the message using that service. Global Cabinets Hub uses details you send to respond to your enquiry. WhatsApp and your email provider handle messages under their own privacy terms.

Offline access

The optional installable website uses browser caching to keep pages and photos available offline. It does not save your quotation or enquiry details.

For privacy questions, email info@globalcabinetshub.co.za.
